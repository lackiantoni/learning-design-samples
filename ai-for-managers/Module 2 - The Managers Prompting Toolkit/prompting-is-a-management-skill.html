Prompting Fundamentals

Prompting Is a Management Skill, Not a Technical Skill

If you think prompting is a technical hurdle, you will under-use AI and feel vaguely inadequate about it. Once you see it as delegation, the barrier drops, because you are already good at delegation. The same things that make instructions to a person clear make instructions to an AI clear.

The Five Elements

Delegation You Already Know How to Give

A strong prompt has the same five parts as a strong delegation. Research shows task-delegation quality is a real predictor of output quality, and the variables that matter most are clarity of the outcome, contextual information, scope constraints, and format of the deliverable.

R

Role

Who the AI should act as. "You are a manager writing a brief performance-file note."

T

Task

The specific thing to produce. "Draft feedback about a missed deadline."

C

Context

The relevant situation. "Mid-level analyst, missed by three days, scope creep both parties failed to flag early."

C

Constraints

The boundaries. "Factual, focus on the behavior not the person, three to four sentences."

F

Format

The shape of the result. "Use the SBI structure: Situation, Behavior, Impact."

Before and After

Same Model, Same Minute, Different Clarity

Weak prompt

"Write feedback for my employee who missed their deadline."

Result: bland filler that could apply to anyone. Vague in, vague out.

Strong prompt

"You are a manager writing a brief performance note. Draft SBI feedback for a mid-level analyst who missed a project deadline by three days. Context: scope crept and neither side flagged it early. Keep it factual and behavior-focused, three to four sentences."

Result: a draft Reza could almost use. Five elements, specific output.

Reza's story

Reza, still skeptical, tries the lazy prompt first: "Write feedback for my employee who missed their deadline." The result is bland filler. Then Reza adds the five elements: role, the specific analyst, the scope-creep context, the constraints, and the SBI format. The second draft is something Reza could almost use. Same model, same minute. The difference was not AI skill. It was the same clarity Reza would give a person.

Apply It Today

Rewrite one vague prompt

Take one vague prompt you have used and rewrite it with all five elements. Run both. The gap between them is your prompting skill ceiling, and it is mostly just clarity.
