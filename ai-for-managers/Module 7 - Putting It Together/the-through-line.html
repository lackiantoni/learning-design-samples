Every Module in One Line

The Same Argument, Six Applications

Foundation

The accurate mental model: AI predicts likely text, which is why it can be confident and wrong, and why the four failure modes (hallucination, automation bias, representational bias, context blindness) are predictable rather than random.

Prompting

Turned prompting into the delegation skill you already have, working from the messy inputs you actually possess. The five elements mirror the five elements of clear delegation.

Coaching

Drew the coaching line: AI preps the manager, the manager runs the conversation, and some conversations stay fully human. The working alliance is yours to keep.

Planning

Compressed planning without outsourcing the judgment of what matters. Always spot-check what AI summarizes. The choice stays yours.

Decisions

Named automation bias as the central risk and gave you procedural habits: risk-sorting, decision patterns, trust calibration, to counter it before it quietly degrades your judgment.

Responsibility

Made you the accountability layer: privacy, bias, transparency, and team norms operationalized into daily practice. Responsible use ran through every module because in real work, those checks happen in the moment or not at all.
