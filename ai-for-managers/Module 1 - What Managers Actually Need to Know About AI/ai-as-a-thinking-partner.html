The Right Model

AI as a Thinking Partner, Not an Oracle

The failure modes could leave you thinking the safe move is to avoid AI. That is the wrong lesson. The right lesson is to use AI for what it is good at and supply what it cannot. Getting this division of labor right is what separates managers who get real value from those who either over-trust the tool or refuse to touch it.

The Division of Labor

What Each Party Owns

The productive model is a partnership with a clear split. Neither role is optional. AI without your judgment produces confident nonsense. Your judgment without AI leaves speed and option-breadth on the table.

AI brings

  • Breadth: more options than you would generate alone
  • Speed: a first draft in seconds, not an hour
  • Synthesis: patterns across large volumes of text
  • Angles you did not initially consider

You bring

  • Organizational context AI has never seen
  • Relationship history and team dynamics
  • Ethical accountability for the outcome
  • Final judgment on what actually matters

In Practice

Sam and the Team Message

Sam's story

Sam needs to draft a message to the team about a sudden deadline shift. AI produces three versions in seconds, each with a different tone. That is the breadth-and-speed contribution, and it is real value. But Sam knows the team is already stretched and that one person is on the edge of burnout, context the AI does not have. Sam picks the warmest version, softens it further, and adds a line acknowledging the strain. The AI gave Sam a running start. Sam gave it the thing that made it land.

Apply It Today

Sort your own work

Take one task you did with AI this week and sort what it contributed (breadth, speed, structure) from what you had to contribute (context, judgment, relationships). Notice that both columns had to be full for the result to be good.

Reflect

  • Where are you currently expecting AI to supply something from your column (judgment, context, accountability)?
  • Where are you doing slow, broad work yourself that AI could accelerate without touching your judgment?
